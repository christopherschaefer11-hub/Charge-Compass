About your map, updates & emergency backup

Stations come from the USDOT/BTS Alternative Fueling Stations layer, sourced from AFDC. “Listed operational” is a directory status, not a real-time guarantee. Connector presence does not guarantee that a specific stall works. Check the operator before committing to a low-battery drive.

Field Club is represented by the country club area, not your exact house. The commute line is a representative OSRM/OpenStreetMap route via I-80 and I-680. Corridor distance is perpendicular proximity, not a driving detour. “Near me” uses your location only after you request it; it stays in this browser.

Refresh fetches and saves the latest Omaha-area directory. The app checks for stale data on opening and while open. The last successful snapshot is retained when refresh fails. Weekly background refresh status is shown below. Favorites and notes are saved on this device only. This private app requires internet/sign-in to reopen; export KML before an emergency.

KML exports the currently filtered stations with separate folders for fast, slow, and access/adapter checks. Import on desktop at Google My Maps → Create map → Import. A KML import is a snapshot, not a live Google Maps saved-place list.

Wagoneer S: CCS1 for DC and J1772 for AC connect directly. NACS/Tesla sites need the appropriate approved adapter plus confirmed site/vehicle access. A DC adapter is not a Level 2 adapter. CHAdeMO-only sites are excluded.

Background refresh: Sunday mornings around 6:00 a.m. Central. Last successful directory fetch is shown above.
